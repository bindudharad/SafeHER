# SafeHER Stage 2: Remaining Issues & Future Roadmap

## 1. Stage 2 Scope Boundaries vs. Future Stages

Stage 2 successfully delivered **genuine, non-mocked evidence analysis** for:
- Official C2PA Cryptographic Content Credentials verification (`c2pa-python`).
- Pretrained facial detection, landmark extraction, and aligned crops (`retinaface.pth`).

In accordance with strict safety, security, and integrity principles, SafeHER did not synthesize or invent artificial scores for models whose authentic pretrained checkpoints have not yet been wired into the pipeline.

- **Enhancement Opportunity**: Integrate outbound signing using `c2pa.Builder` with SafeHER's X.509 authority certificate to cryptographically sign generated Section 63/65B forensic court bundles.

## 3. Maintenance & Operational Considerations

1. **Model Cache Memory**: The RetinaFace model consumes ~280 MB of RAM while resident in memory. For low-resource VPS deployments (<1 GB RAM), container memory limits should be configured to at least 1.5 GB.
2. **C2PA Format Coverage**: While standard formats (JPEG, PNG, WebP) are fully supported, older legacy formats (BMP, raw GIF) will return `unsupported_format`. The frontend gracefully renders an informative badge indicating that C2PA credentials are not applicable to the format.
3. **Continuous Dependency Auditing**: Keep `c2pa-python` updated as CAI and C2PA standards evolve to incorporate newly approved cryptographic signature algorithms.
